HAUS CRM — Design System

Linear/Attio minimalism carrying the HAUS crimson. Every value here is a token in tokens.css. Stone neutrals, hairline structure, mono numerals, one accent. Thai-first UI; "Lead" stays English.

Foundations

Color

The logo maroon is the brand anchor only — the app accent is a brighter step of the same hue. Crimson is the single accent; neutrals are warm stone (never beige); status hues are reserved for state.

Two hue tiers. Status colors are darker (text-safe, ≥4.5:1). Stage dots are vivid and live in dots/marks only — they fail small-text contrast. Never recolor a stage by rank; assign in fixed order.
